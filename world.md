# Shattered Covenant — World & Narrative Overview

## 1. High Concept & Premise
***Shattered Covenant*** (working title; alternate: ***The Four Edicts***) is a classic turn-based fantasy JRPG with a grounded, high-fantasy *World of Warcraft* aesthetic. 

For millennia, the continent of **Vaelora** was bound together by **The Edict Pillar (The Covenant Stone)**—a colossal divine monolith carved by the ancient Titans at the center of the world to balance the continent's ley-lines and govern the laws of magic.

When the Edict Pillar is mysteriously shattered, its four massive **Great Shards** crash into ancient, sealed **Titan Vaults** across the continent, while its palm-sized core—the **Heart-Shard**—falls from the sky into the frontier quarry town of **Oakhaven**, fusing to the gauntlet of a young apprentice Runic Mason named **Kaelen Vance**. Now, the continent's three great neighboring superpowers are marching to war to claim the Four Great Shards and rewrite the magical laws of the world in their favor.

---

## 2. The Three Neighboring Superpowers (The Realms of Vaelora)

Rather than time-traveling across eras, the continent is divided into three massive, distinct territories—each drawing power from one of the three primal aspects of the Covenant Stone:

| Territory | Magic Domain | Culture & Warcraft-Style Aesthetic | Visual & Musical Palette |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Mythic Dawn** *(The Dawn Wilds)* | 🔴 **Dawn (Primal / Sun / Nature)** | Towering world-trees, golden Titan sun-ziggurats, wild gods, sun-scale wyvern riders, and fierce oathbound clans. | Lush emerald canopies, deep amber gold, overgrown ruins; orchestral woodwinds & war-drums. |
| **The Obsidian Reign** *(The Obsidian Empire)* | 🟡 **Obsidian (Forge / Earth / Edict)** | A fortress-empire of volcanic blackrock citadels, dark-iron magma foundries, runic war-golems, and Inquisitorial Scribes. | Volcanic glass blacks, molten brass, crimson forge-fire; driving cello & anvil percussion. |
| **The Astral Wastes** *(The Astral Badlands)* | 🔵 **Astral (Arcane / Star / Void)** | Shattered crystalline deserts, floating mana-spires, purple/cyan arcane storms, and Ethereal scholars wrapped in runic bindings. | Stark white sands, cyan/violet crystal neon, starlit rifts; haunting synth, harp & piano melodies. |

* **The Central Neutral Hub — The Grand Archive:** Built directly around the crater of the shattered Edict Pillar where the three borders meet, governed by the scholarly order of the **High Scribes**.

## 3. Core Exploration & World Mechanics

### Wardstone Attunement (Field & Dungeon Puzzles)
Across towns and dungeons stand ancient **Local Wardstones** that regulate regional ley-lines. Using the **Heart-Shard Gauntlet**, Kaelen can channel **Dawn**, **Obsidian**, or **Astral** energy into a Wardstone to physically transform the environment:
* **Dawn Attunement:** Causes giant sun-vines and roots to erupt, forming bridges across chasms or cracking open sealed stone doors.
* **Obsidian Attunement:** Cools molten lava rivers into walkable basalt platforms or powers up dormant Titan forge-elevators.
* **Astral Attunement:** Activates low-gravity starlight currents and levitates sunken ruins into the air.

### World Traversal: Titan Waygates & Sun-Drakes
* **Titan Waygates:** Ancient dormant teleportation arches scattered across the three territories. Only Kaelen's Heart-Shard gauntlet can re-inscribe their runes, unlocking fast travel between discovered regions and serving as full-rest checkpoints.
* **Tamed Sun-Drakes:** Earned in **The Mythic Dawn** during Act II, allowing the party to soar over mountain ridges, canyons, and the central sea to reach high-altitude dungeons and hidden Titan sanctuaries.

### Tri-Realm Weapon Forging (Signature Sidequests)
Instead of disposable shop weapons, major sidequests revolve around **Tri-Realm Forging**—taking an ancient dormant weapon frame and tempering it in the sacred fires of the **Obsidian Reign**, infusing it with living amber in the **Mythic Dawn**, and etching star-runes into its core in the **Astral Wastes**.

## 4. The 3-Act Narrative Arc

### Act I: The Quarry Fall (6–8 Hours)
* **The Opening (Ley-Line Cataclysm):** You begin in **Oakhaven**, a rugged mining and masonry town in the **Frontier**, the border region between **The Obsidian Reign** and the Dawn Wilds. While apprentice mason **Kaelen Vance** and his mentor **Master Orin** inspect the quarry's boundary stone, the central Edict Pillar shatters in the distant capital. A blazing fragment—the **Heart-Shard**—falls from the sky directly into Oakhaven's quarry.
* **The Inciting Incident:** Master Orin pushes Kaelen clear of the impact and is petrified inside a pillar of volatile rune-crystal, while the Heart-Shard fuses permanently into Kaelen's mason gauntlet. Within hours, vanguard forces from both the **Mythic Dawn** and the **Obsidian Reign's Inquisition** descend on Oakhaven to seize the shard.
* **The Goal:** Kaelen forms an uneasy truce with **Sura**, a Sun-Scale Valkyrie scout sent by the Mythic Dawn, after they discover that only the **Grand Archive** possesses the knowledge to safely unseal Master Orin's crystal without killing him—and that four colossal **Great Shards** have fallen into ancient Titan Vaults across the continent.

### Act II: The Four Vaults Race (15–20 Hours)
* **The World Opens Up:** At the **Grand Archive**, Kaelen and Sura are joined by **Vesper**, an Ethereal Star-Bound Mystic from the Astral Wastes whose mortal body was transmuted into living starlight during the Pillar's shattering, and **Cassian Thorne**, a defector Imperial Inkwright.
* **The Objective:** **High Archon Seraphine Thorne** tasks the party with a desperate mission: use Kaelen's Heart-Shard gauntlet (the only key that can open the ancient Titan seals) to enter the **Four Ancient Titan Vaults** and secure the **Four Great Shards** before the warring armies of the Dawn, Reign, and Wastes breach them and plunge Vaelora into a genocidal war:
  1. **The Sun-Root Ziggurat** *(The Mythic Dawn)*
  2. **The Halls of the First Anvil** *(The Obsidian Reign)*
  3. **The Star-Glass Nexus** *(The Astral Wastes)*
  4. **The Sunken Reliquary** *(The Contested Tri-Border Maelstrom)*
* **The Midpoint Twist:** When the party returns all Four Great Shards to the Grand Archive after surviving the four-way clash at the Sunken Reliquary, **High Archon Seraphine Thorne** betrays them. Vesper's sealed memories shatter open: Seraphine *intentionally* destroyed the Edict Pillar herself because the ancient Titan seals prevented anyone from rewriting its laws while intact. She used Kaelen's gauntlet to unseal the four Titan Vaults and gather the awakened shards so she could carve a **New Covenant**—one that strips free will and enslaves all three realms under her eternal dominion.

### Act III: The Unwritten Covenant (8–10 Hours)
* **The Climax:** Seraphine raises **The Spire of the First Word** from the Archive's crater, casting an oppressive Edict across the sky. With their mentors, oaths, and pasts on the line, Kaelen, Sura, Vesper, and Cassian travel across all three territories on their Sun-Drakes to unite the fractured leaders of the **Mythic Dawn**, **Obsidian Reign**, and **Astral Wastes** into a **Tri-Realm Coalition**.
* **The Ending:** While the coalition armies clash with Seraphine's Edict Constructs at the base of the Spire, the four heroes ascend to the pinnacle. In the final battle, Kaelen uses the Heart-Shard not to reassemble the Edict Pillar, but to **shatter its dominion forever**—freeing Master Orin and releasing the continent's ley-lines so that magic and fate belong to the mortal world, unwritten and unbound.
