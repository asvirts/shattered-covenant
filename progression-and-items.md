# Progression, Talent Trees, Equipment & Glyph System

## 1. Character Progression Overview
Character growth combines three interlocking systems inspired by *World of Warcraft* and classic turn-based JRPGs:
1. **2-Branch Class Talent Trees:** Spend Talent Points earned on level-up to specialize each character's role (freely respeccable at any **Titan Waygate**).
2. **The Glyph Tablet (4–6 Socket Grid):** Slot **Dawn**, **Obsidian**, and **Astral** Glyphs chiselled from Titan ruins to unlock cross-realm spells and **Adjacency Synergy Combos**.
3. **Evolving Signature Weapons + Looted Armor & Trinkets:** Reforge each hero's iconic weapon at Master Forges across the three territories while looting build-defining Armor and **WoW-style Trinkets** from dungeons and bosses.

## 3. The Glyph Tablet (4–6 Socket Grid)

Instead of an oversized spreadsheet grid, each character carries a focused **4-socket (expanding to 6-socket) Covenant Tablet** arranged in a linked geometric rune pattern (`2x2` expanding to `2x3`).

### The Three Glyph Categories
* 🔴 **Dawn Glyphs (Wilds / Sun):** `[EMBER]`, `[GALE]`, `[FANG]`, `[THORN]`
* 🟡 **Obsidian Glyphs (Forge / Edict):** `[FORGE]`, `[SHRAPNEL]`, `[EDICT]`, `[BULWARK]`
* 🔵 **Astral Glyphs (Star / Arcane):** `[HASTE]`, `[ENTROPY]`, `[ECHO]`, `[STASIS]`

### Adjacency Synergy Combos (Dual-Domain Fusions)
Slotting two compatible Glyphs in connected adjacent sockets fuses their ley-lines to unlock a **Synergy Skill**:
* 🔴 **`[EMBER]`** + 🟡 **`[FORGE]`** = **`[MAGMA ERUPTION]`** *(AoE Fire/Blunt damage + melts enemy `GRT` by 25%)*
* 🔴 **`[THORN]`** + 🟡 **`[BULWARK]`** = **`[IRONWOOD AEGIS]`** *(Party barrier that reflects physical damage and roots attackers)*
* 🔴 **`[FANG]`** + 🔵 **`[ECHO]`** = **`[PHANTOM PACK]`** *(High-crit physical strike that repeats automatically at the start of next turn)*
* 🟡 **`[SHRAPNEL]`** + 🔵 **`[ENTROPY]`** = **`[CORROSIVE BARRAGE]`** *(Multi-hit volley that permanently removes 1 max Stability segment from the boss)*
* 🟡 **`[EDICT]`** + 🔵 **`[STASIS]`** = **`[ABSOLUTE DECREE]`** *(Freezes an enemy's turn portrait and amplifies the next single hit they take by +50%)*
* 🔴 **`[GALE]`** + 🔵 **`[HASTE]`** = **`[TAILWIND CONVERGENCE]`** *(Pulls all 4 party members forward on the CTB timeline)*

### Cross-Character Dual Techs (Resonance Links)
When two party members equip the **same Synergy Combo** on their tablets and their portraits land adjacent on the CTB Timeline, they can execute a **Resonance Link (Dual Tech)**—combining both turns into a cinematic team ultimate that deals massive damage and cracks multiple Stability segments.

## 4. Equipment: Evolving Weapons, Armor & WoW-Style Trinkets

Each character has **4 Equipment Slots**:
1. **Signature Weapon (Evolving across 5 Tiers):**
   * Upgraded at Master Forges using boss cores and realm ores rather than replaced by generic shop swords.
   * *Example (Kaelen's Maul & Gauntlet):*
     1. *Apprentice Quarry Maul* (Act I start)
     2. *Runebound Basalt Breaker* (Forged in Oakhaven)
     3. *Anvil-Lord's Crusher* (Forged in the Halls of the First Anvil)
     4. *Titan-Forged Worldbreaker* (Tri-Realm Sidequest Forge)
     5. *Gavel of the Unwritten* (Act III Ultimate)
2. **Armor Slot (Helm/Chest Set):** Provides base `VIT`, `GRT`, and `WRD`, plus elemental resistances.
3. **Trinket Slot 1 & Trinket Slot 2 (*World of Warcraft*-Style Build-Enablers):**
   * Trinkets dropped by dungeon bosses and elite hunts have unique, build-defining passive or active effects rather than flat stats:
     * ***Vial of Living Sun-Sap*** *(Sun-Root Ziggurat Drop):* "Whenever the wielder heals an ally, grant them 1 stack of *Sunfire Thorns*."
     * ***Core of the Magma Golem*** *(Halls of the First Anvil Drop):* "Every 3rd physical attack unleashes a molten shockwave that chips +1 Stability on all foes."
     * ***Pendulum of the Star-Seer*** *(Star-Glass Nexus Drop):* "Reduces the `INS` cost of all Synergy Combo skills by 25%."
     * ***Master Orin's Mason Compass*** *(Act I Keepsake):* "Striking the Heart-Shard for a Domain Shift grants the wielder a 20% Max HP shield."
